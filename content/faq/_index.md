### Who is this event for?
Anybody involved in data analytics: data scientists, data engineers, analysts, information designers, infrastructure gurus and product managers. along with open source communities and leaders.

### Will the event be in-person or online?
OSA CON 2026 will take place in person in San Francisco and will be live-streamed online, allowing participants from around the world to join virtually.

### Is the event free?
Yes, participation in OSA CON 2026 is free.

### How can I register for the event?
Pre-registration is open. [Sign up here](https://luma.com/eis7567t).

### Is there a code of conduct?
Yes. Please review our [Code of Conduct](/coc).

### I am interested in sponsoring. How can I learn more?
We'd love to hear from you. Reach out to hello@osacon.io to learn more about sponsorship opportunities and how your organization can get involved with OSA CON 2026.
